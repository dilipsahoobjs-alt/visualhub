Key Mobility Insights

  • Hill States & Sprawls: High car ownership in Goa (45.2%), J&K (23.7%), and HP (22.1%) is heavily influenced by geography, terrain, and tourism density.
  • The Urban Core Effect: Maharashtra (28.5% PT) and West Bengal (26.0% PT) leverage suburban commuter rail networks to move millions efficiently.
  • Non-Car Dependent Hubs: States like UP, Bihar, and Odisha show low car ownership (<6%) yet low formal public commute share (<13%), pointing to high 2-wheeler and informal transit usage.
Data Sources: NFHS-5 (2019–21) Household Car Ownership % & Census 2011 Public Transport Commute Share (Bus + Train).
